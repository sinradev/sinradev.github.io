Two workflows

Writing and development advance in parallel

Every other tool gives an issue one status. Sinra gives it two: where the specification stands, and where the code stands. You build both lists yourself.

  • Product writes while developers build
  • No issue waits on a status that means two things at once
  • A spec can be finished long before the branch is
Capacity

You find out someone is overloaded before the cycle starts

Sinra adds up estimated time per person and per team and compares it to the availability you set. The warning arrives during planning.

  • Availability per person, per team, in percent
  • Estimated against real capacity, per cycle
  • The overflow is shown, not hidden in a total
Specification

Spec writing has its own board

Product owners track specifications the way developers track code, grouped by capability, filtered by release.

  • Columns by writing status
  • Grouped by capability, so a late feature is obvious
  • Drag a card to change its status
Quality

Tests are attached to the thing they check

A test case points at a capability and its acceptance criteria. A release cannot claim to be ready while its tests say otherwise.

  • Reusable test templates
  • A named tester, with their workload counted
  • Acceptance recorded against the release
