Concepts

Understand the core concepts behind Sinra: cycles, capabilities, testings, releases, and more.

Cycles
Cycles are Sinra's time-boxed work periods. Learn how they differ from traditional sprints and why they fit V-Model + Agile teams.
Issues
Issues are the fundamental unit of work in Sinra. Every task, bug, and specification is an issue. Learn how they work, what fields they carry, and how the dual-status workflow tracks writing and development separately.
Capabilities
Capabilities are Sinra's equivalent of epics, but with a key difference: a capability has a clear completion state. Learn how capabilities connect releases, issues, and QA into a traceable delivery unit.
Releases
A release in Sinra is a scope commitment, not just a deployment event. Learn how releases define what you deliver, connect to cycles and capabilities, and communicate progress to your whole organization.
Capacity Management
Sinra's capacity system makes the fundamental project equation visible: what you want to deliver vs. what your team can actually do. Learn how availability rates, cycle capacity, and workload calculations work.
Sync Events
Sync events are Sinra's structured team synchronization moments: planning meetings, retrospectives, phase gate reviews, and post-mortems. Each has a precise role in the project lifecycle.

Core concepts that power the Sinra platform.
