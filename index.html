Does your team look like this?

These aren't talent problems. They show up in every team that grew faster than its way of working.

  • Deadlines slip every sprint, and nobody knows why. Cycles are sized to each person's real availability, so overload shows before the cycle starts.
  • A feature is "done" but nobody tested it before production. Releases can't close until every linked test is accepted or explicitly waived.
  • A week before release, nobody can say what's shipping. Every issue is tied to a capability and a release, so the answer is one screen away.

It's a method problem. sinra ships the method.
