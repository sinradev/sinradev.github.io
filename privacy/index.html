2. Data We Collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the following personal data:

  • Full name

We do not use any analytics tools or tracking scripts. No cookies are set by sinra.dev.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis

Your data is collected exclusively to respond to your inquiry.

Legal basis: Your explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given by checking the consent checkbox on our contact form.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for a maximum of 3 years from the date of your last contact with us. After this period, your data is permanently deleted.

5. Data Recipients

Your contact form submission is processed through our application platform at app.sinra.dev. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Right to portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: support@sinra.dev
